Most legacy systems carry decades of business logic that nobody fully understands anymore. The original developers are gone, the documentation is incomplete, and making changes feels risky.

Replai reads your existing code - COBOL, Oracle Forms, PL/I, and others - and builds a structured map of what the system actually does: the business rules, the workflows, the dependencies between components. Your team can then ask plain-language questions about that code, trace how changes will ripple through the system, and plan modernization work without guessing.

The result is a team that can safely extend, refactor, or migrate legacy systems without needing someone who remembers how things were built 30 years ago.

PAYCALC.cbl COBOL · 1987
  1. CALC-NET-PAY.
  2. COMPUTE WS-GROSS =
  3. WS-HOURS * WS-RATE.
  4. IF WS-HOURS > 40
  5. COMPUTE WS-GROSS = WS-GROSS +
  6. (WS-HOURS - 40) * WS-RATE * 0.5
  7. END-IF.
  8. COMPUTE WS-NET-PAY =
  9. WS-GROSS - WS-TAX - WS-DEDUCT.
  10. IF WS-NET-PAY < ZERO
  11. MOVE ZERO TO WS-NET-PAY
  12. PERFORM WRITE-AUDIT-REC
  13. END-IF.
  14. PERFORM POST-LEDGER-ENTRY.
