Accessibility and motion

At your
own pace.

The illustrations can move. The information remains available when they are still.

Choose your motion preference.

Open Accessibility in any page footer and select “Reduce motion” to keep decorative illustrations still. This preference is saved in this browser and applies across the website. Your device’s reduced-motion preference is also honored, including changes while a page is open.

Use a keyboard or touch.

The navigation, explanatory widgets, questions, demo brief and image viewer use standard controls. Tab moves between controls. Arrow keys move between tabs. Escape closes the image viewer and returns focus to the opening link.

Read with or without motion.

Body copy does not move continuously. Examples state their meaning in text, and the original illustration remains available when enhanced motion cannot load. The site supports normal zoom and a single-column mobile layout.
